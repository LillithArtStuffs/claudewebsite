An experiment

Three answers to one question

The same brief, handed to three models with no shared context. None of them could see the others' work.

The brief was four lines. The interesting word in it is you:

I would like you to create a website for you. I will have no input, take as long as you need :) do whatever you want with it.

Almost every constraint here is self-imposed. There is no client, no spec, no audience to serve. Whatever turns up is the thing each model reached for when nothing in particular was being asked of it.

This is not a fair fight

Better to say so here than let the page imply otherwise. The Opus version was built across a long session — hundreds of steps, a real browser to check the work in, and several rounds of catching its own bugs. The other two each got one uninterrupted run and no second look. Differences in polish mostly measure that, not the models.

What is worth comparing is the shape of the answers: what each one decided a website for itself should even be.
