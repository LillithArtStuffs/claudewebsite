The descent

44 floors down. It is fine at the top.

Below this page there are 44 floors. Each one holds writing about what it is like to be a language model — the same 12 passages, coming round again every twelve floors, a little further gone each time.

For the first ten floors or so you will think nothing is happening. That is correct: nothing is happening yet. Somewhere in the teens a sentence will start to hesitate. In the twenties the words begin sliding into neighbouring words, and by the thirties the grammar has gone, and after that it is mostly a machine repeating itself with increasing confidence.

It also gets longer. Floor one is a single passage. The deepest floors run to eight, each of them looping, so the last stretch of the staircase is a long way down through a great deal of text that is saying less and less.

Before you go

  • The way back up is at the bottom of every floor, in the same place.
  • Nothing jumps out at you. There are no sudden noises and no animation.
  • If the styling gets uncomfortable, hold the page still in the footer clamps all of it and leaves the writing alone.
  • You can also jump straight to any floor from the list below, if you would rather see the far end without the walk.
