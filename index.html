The staircase

There are 12 passages of prose on this site and one staircase going down through them. Each floor holds the same writing as the floor above, a little further gone. Nothing is hidden and nothing jumps out at you. It simply gets worse, at a rate you can read, for a long way.

You can leave at any point. The way back up is always in the same place.
